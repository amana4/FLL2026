## Precision tokens

| Left | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Points | 50 | 50 | 35 | 25 | 15 | 10 | 0 |

**The first interruption is free.** The second costs 15, the third 10, the fourth 10.
So one rescue is nothing; three are 25 points.

## Before the match

- [ ] Hub charged. **Battery level changes how far the robot drives.**
- [ ] Colour thresholds re-measured **at this venue**, under this lighting
- [ ] Correct program in the correct slot
- [ ] Attachments laid out in run order, on the correct side
- [ ] Robot in the launch area, gyro reset while stationary
- [ ] Dock choice decided: which of M13, M14, M15 on mine, city, farm
- [ ] Ask the opposing team about **M07** — both teams score if both fully extend

## The No Equipment Constraint

**M01 · M04 · M05 · M07 · M09 · M10 · M12 · M15**

Those eight score **nothing** if the model is touching any of your equipment when the
match ends. Not on: M02, M03, M06, M08, M11, M13, M14.

Last action of every run: make sure nothing is left touching a model.

## Rules that decide matches

- **Wording means precisely and only what it says.** If a detail is not mentioned, it
  does not matter.
- **Unclear calls go to the team.** Benefit of the doubt is yours — so ask.
- Requirements must be **visibly met at the end** of the match.
- "In" an area includes the **lines and the airspace above it**.
- Technicians may only handle things **completely inside their own home area**.
- **Nothing passes between the two home areas** during a match.
- Interrupting the robot outside home costs one token, and it must be relaunched.
- Objects picked up outside home after the last launch **go to the referee**.
- You may not take models apart, separate the Dual Lock, or break a model.
- A model joined to the robot must come free **in one motion, undamaged**, if asked.
- Do not touch the other team's field. Points they lose to your interference
  **score for them anyway**.

## Equipment limits

One controller. **Four motors.** Touch/force, colour, distance and gyro sensors only.
Everything must fit within the two launch areas, under **12 in. (305 mm)** — and
fitting it all into **one** launch area is worth 20 points.

One sheet of notebook paper per home area is allowed and does not count as equipment.

## Gracious Professionalism is scored

Referees score every match: **Accomplished 3** by default, **Exceeds 4**,
**Developing 2**. It feeds the Core Values score.

Robot broken? Turn up and explain what happened — that still scores 2 to 4. Not
turning up scores zero.

## Judging: one session, about 30 minutes

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 2 min | Welcome, and you set everything out |
| 5 min | Innovation Project presentation |
| 5 min | Questions |
| 5 min | Robot Design explanation |
| 5 min | Questions |
| up to 8 min | Final thoughts and feedback |

**All five students attend.** There is **no field in the room** — explain the robot
from photos and the design log. You set up once, at the start.

## If it goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Hub will not connect | Restart hub, then use USB, then restart the laptop |
| Program missing | Reload from `code/spike-projects/` |
| Attachment broke | Spare parts box. Fix between matches, not during. |
| Failed inspection | Ask the inspector exactly what is wrong. Usually size. |
| Robot dead mid-match | Stop it in place. No token lost if you are not relaunching. |
| Running late | Tell a volunteer straight away. They can usually help. |
| Someone upset | Step away from the pit together. It is a Saturday. |

## Remember

Only your **best of three matches** counts. A bad first match costs nothing.
