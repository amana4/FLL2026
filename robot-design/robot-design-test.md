# Robot Design Test

Do this after you have read [Our Robot, Explained](our-robot-explained.md).
Keep that page open in another tab. Looking things up is allowed, and it is the
point: you learn the answer by finding it.

The test has two parts.

**Part 1: Quick quiz.** Twelve multiple-choice questions about our robot, our
code and the rubric. The form marks them as soon as you press Submit and shows
your score. If you get one wrong, read the hint, go back to the page, and take
the test again. You can take it as many times as you like.

**Part 2: Explain it.** Open questions with no right answer to look up. They
are the "Team to answer" boxes from the knowledge base. The coach reads every
answer before the brainstorm. Where your answers differ from each other, that
is what the brainstorm will sort out.

Write in your own words, the way you would say it to a judge. Short is fine.
"I don't know" is a good answer if it is true, because it tells us what to cover
in class.

## What the open questions ask

So you can think about them before you open the form:

**IDENTIFY**

- Which missions does our robot try, and why those?
- Name one mission we skipped, and the reason: points, time, or risk.
- Where did we get help or ideas? For example videos, other teams, building
  instructions, or coaches.

**DESIGN**

- What did you personally build, and what did you code?
- How did the team share out the work?

**CREATE**

- Pick one attachment. What does it do, and what did we change from the
  original design?
- Why did we stop using the colour sensors, and what do we use to steer instead?
- `Phase 5.py` does four jobs: "Pull", "open door", "ant" and "Tree support".
  Which mission number is each one?

**ITERATE**

- Name something that broke or did not work. What did we change, and what
  happened after?
- How do we know a run is reliable? Give a number if you know one.

**COMMUNICATE**

- Explain our robot to a judge in three sentences.
- What are you most proud of?

**And one more:** what do you not know yet, that we should sort out in class?
