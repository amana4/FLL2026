## Guidance

- **Students write it.** Judges can tell when an adult did. Rough student writing
  scores better than polished adult writing.
- **Show the mess.** Sketches, crossed-out ideas, photos of broken attachments.
- **Date everything.** It demonstrates the progression.
- **Photos over paragraphs.** A photo of v1 next to v3 says more than a page of text.
- **Check the page limit** before designing the layout.
