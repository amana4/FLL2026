## The story

> Many creatures thrive just below the surface. Flip the rock to discover what's living underneath but leave no trace by placing it back the way you found it.

## Scoring conditions

| Condition | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The research flag is down | **20** |
| **Bonus:** and the rock has been returned to its original starting position | **10 added** |

**Maximum: 30 points**

## Notes and gotchas

Mission model: Research Flag, rock.

The bonus requires putting the rock *back* — that's a second robot action, not a side effect of the first. Budget time for it or skip the bonus deliberately.
