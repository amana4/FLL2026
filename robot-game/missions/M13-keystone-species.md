## The story

> Keystone species bring balance to their ecosystem and shape the forest around them. Your team must build and deliver a keystone species to this newly protected habitat.

## Scoring conditions

| Condition | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Your team's keystone species is on the restoration platform, and the young trees are raised | **30** |

**Maximum: 30 points**

## Notes and gotchas

Mission models: Restoration Platform, Young Trees.

**Uses the new interchangeable dock system** (see Docks below).

**You build the keystone species yourself** from bag 20:

> You can use the bricks in bag 20 to build your keystone species model. Your keystone species counts as equipment and will be included in the pre-match inspection. Teams will be asked to identify their keystone species at that time.

Because it's equipment, it counts toward the 12 in. height limit and the one-launch-area inspection bonus (Mission 20). Build it small.

This is also a nice Innovation Project tie-in — the team chooses which keystone species to represent and can explain why.
