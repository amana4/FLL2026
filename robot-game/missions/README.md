## Equipment Inspection — 20 free points

> If your robot and all equipment fit completely in **one** launch area and
> under a height limit of **12 in. (305 mm)** during inspection: **20 points**

The cheapest points on the field. It costs nothing but discipline about how
much stuff you bring and how big your attachments are.

Note this interacts with **M13** — your self-built keystone species counts as
equipment, so it eats into the same budget. Build it compact.

## Precision Tokens — up to 50 points

You start with **six tokens worth 50 points**. Interrupt the robot outside home
and the referee removes one.

| Tokens left | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Points** | 50 | 50 | 35 | 25 | 15 | 10 | 0 |

**The first interruption is free** — 6 and 5 tokens both score 50. After that
it gets expensive fast: the second costs 15 points, the third 10, the fourth 10.

This reframes reliability as a scoring mechanism, not just a convenience. A run
that needs two rescues costs you 25 points before you score anything.

## First things to notice

**M10 Fragile Microhabitats is 20 points for doing nothing.** Both habitats
start in their correct position; you keep the points by *not* disturbing them.
It appears in two places on the field map, so check it against every route.

**M08 Tangled is 30 points for one condition** — the best single-action value
on the field.

**M04 Lucky Leaves can score zero.** If the katydid ends up outside the leaf
habitat, even partly, you lose all 30 points. High risk, needs a precise
attachment.

**M07 Humongous Fungus needs the opposing team.** Both teams get the bonus if
both fully extend. Mention it to your match partner beforehand — free points
for both of you.

**M13–M15 use the new interchangeable docks.** You choose which model goes on
the mine, city, and farm dock *before* the match. M15's bonus depends on
matching the dock to the action your robot does most reliably. Decide this
deliberately.

## Filling in the analysis

Each mission file has an analysis table to complete in Weeks 3–4:

1. Build the model (bag numbers are in the table above)
2. Estimate the time your robot needs
3. Compute points-per-second — that, not enthusiasm, drives the run plan
4. Roll up into [`../scoring-tracker.md`](../scoring-tracker.md)
5. Group into runs in [`../strategy.md`](../strategy.md)
