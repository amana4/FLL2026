## The story

> Once a towering giant, this aging tree now bends beneath its own weight. Add a support to stabilize the tree for years to come.

## Scoring conditions

| Condition | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The cane is completely raised, touching the tree | **20** |
| The support tie is around the post | **10** |

**Maximum: 30 points**

## Notes and gotchas

Mission models: Cane, Support Tie, Post.

Two independent conditions. The support tie is likely the fiddlier of the two — 'around the post' suggests a threading or dropping action.

### No Equipment Constraint applies to this mission

This mission carries the crossed-out-brick symbol, so:

> "A mission model cannot earn points if it is touching equipment at the end of
> the match."

If the robot leaves an attachment, a wheel or anything else touching this model
when the match ends, the mission scores nothing even if it looks completed. Check
it at the end of every practice run.
