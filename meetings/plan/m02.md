## Agenda

Ninety minutes fits three things, not five. The expert emails come first because
they are the only item with a multi-week fuse — everything else here can slip a
week without cost.

### 6:00–6:25 · Expert list and emails

**Do this first, before anyone touches LEGO.** If the building starts first, this
will not happen.

As a group, list **10 people or organisations** to contact, then have two students
send the emails from the template in
[`expert-outreach.md`](../../innovation-project/expert-outreach.md) before the
meeting ends. Sent in the room, not as homework — homework is where this item died
last time.

Who to approach: university biology or ecology departments (PhD students reply
fastest), the council ecology officer, wildlife trusts, nature reserve wardens,
botanic gardens, zoo or aquarium education officers, beekeepers, foresters.

Why the urgency: replies take two to three weeks, and the Innovation Project
rubric wants feedback from **multiple** people to score Accomplished. Emails sent
tonight become interviews in September. Emails sent in October become nothing.

- [ ] 10 names listed in `expert-outreach.md`
- [ ] Emails actually sent, from a student's own words

### 6:25–7:05 · Build the mission models

Split by bag number so everyone builds something. Build them **exactly** as the
instructions show — a model built wrong teaches the robot the wrong thing, and you
will not find out until it fails at an event.

The bag numbers per mission are in
[`../../robot-game/missions/README.md`](../../robot-game/missions/README.md).

- [ ] Models built, checked against the instructions

### 7:05–7:25 · Biodiversity brainstorm

Run the wide round properly. Target **20-plus ideas**, no criticism, everything
written down.

Give each student a rainforest layer and its official Project Spark — the table is
in [`brainstorm.md`](../../innovation-project/brainstorm.md). Five minutes alone,
then share. It stops five kids converging on bees or plastic, and it guarantees
somebody thinks about soil and fungi.

- [ ] 20+ ideas captured in `brainstorm.md`
