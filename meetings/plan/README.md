## What goes where

These pages are the **plan** — what you intend to do.

The **record** of what actually happened goes in [`meetings/`](../README.md) as
`YYYY-MM-DD.md`, from [`_TEMPLATE.md`](../_TEMPLATE.md). Keep both: the plan
tells you what's next, the record is what the Engineering Notebook is built from.
