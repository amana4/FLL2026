> effective hour of the season.

## Universal advice

**Everyone talks.** Judges track who spoke. If one student answers everything, the
team loses points no matter how good the answers are. Agree in advance roughly who
covers what — then be ready to flex, because judges pick who they ask.

**"I don't know" is an acceptable answer** if followed by something real: "I don't
know the exact number, but _Name_ measured it — she can tell you," or "we didn't
test that, and I'd want to before saying."

**Bring the evidence.** Notebook, design log, photos of failed versions. Showing
beats telling.

**Talk about failures.** Every rubric rewards iteration. A team that says "this is
version four, here's why versions one through three didn't work" outscores a team
with a flawless story and no history.
