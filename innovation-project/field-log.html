Bee or not a bee?

Bees have four wings, long elbowed antennae, rounder bodies, and carry visible pollen loads. Flies have two wings, short thick antennae, big eyes at the front, and hover. Wasps look tougher, are barely hairy, and their stripes are on the shell, not made of hairs.

Honey beeHB

Medium to large, orange-brown to nearly black, striped abdomen, flat plates on hind legs carrying moist clumps of pollen. Flies methodically flower to flower. Telling honey bees from everything else is the single most important call you make.

Bumble beeBUMBLE

Medium to very large, robust and entirely fuzzy, black with yellow stripes. Low buzzing sound. Note the face colour and abdomen tip.

Large carpenter beeCARPENTER

Very large and shiny — like a bumble bee that lost its hair. Usually black or dark blue; males can be golden brown. Often territorial near your head.

Hairy leg beeHLB

Medium to large, short velvety hair especially on the thorax, dry pollen often all over the body. Fast, smooth figure-eight flight. Males can have very long antennae.

Green sweat beeGSB

Medium, metallic green, narrow body, fast flier. Pollen on the hind legs.

Striped sweat beeSSB

Small to medium, narrow, looks black with faint creamy or grey stripes. Often crawls inside flowers. Jagged fast movements.

Small dark beeSDB

Small and narrow. Black, metallic grey-green, blue or brown. Faint stripes if any. Crawls deep into flowers, may swarm near them.

Striped hairy belly beeSHBB

Black with white or silvery hairs and brushes of hair under the abdomen. Often lifts its abdomen at flowers, showing the pollen underneath.

Metallic hairy belly beeMHBB

Small to medium, stout and round, bright metallic blue or green. Pollen brushes under the abdomen, no bold hair bands.

Cuckoo beeCUCKOO

Barely hairy, wasp-like markings made of short thick hairs. Red or black legs. Carries no pollen at all — it lays eggs in other bees' nests.

Only count real visits

Record an insect only if it is on the reproductive parts of a flower — collecting pollen or nectar. Skip anything resting on petals, leaves or stems, or just flying past. Walk so your shadow trails behind you.
