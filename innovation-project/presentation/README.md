# Innovation Project Presentation

**5 minutes maximum**, then judges ask questions. Verify the exact time limit in
this season's rubric — going over gets cut off mid-sentence.

## Structure

A 5-minute shape that covers every rubric point:

| Time | Section | Who presents | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Hook + team intro | _TODO_ | Grab attention, say who we are |
| 0:30–1:15 | The problem | _TODO_ | What it is, who it hurts, why it matters |
| 1:15–2:15 | Research | _TODO_ | Sources, **who we interviewed**, what we learned |
| 2:15–3:15 | Our solution | _TODO_ | What it is, how it works, show the model |
| 3:15–4:15 | Iteration | _TODO_ | Who we shared with, what they said, **what we changed** |
| 4:15–5:00 | Impact + close | _TODO_ | Who benefits, what's next, thank you |

**Everyone speaks.** A presentation with one narrator loses Core Values points.

## Rules of thumb

- Show the prototype — don't just describe it.
- Say the interviewee's name and role out loud. It's concrete and memorable.
- Practice the *handoffs* between speakers; that's where teams stumble.
- Rehearse without notes. Cards are allowed but reading from them flattens it.
- Time every rehearsal. Teams routinely run 7 minutes on the first try.
